Yard and Dock Doors Simulator

Trucks arrive and, if a dock door is free, go straight to it; otherwise they wait in a separate yard area with its own finite capacity. A truck is diverted — a service failure — only when every dock door is busy and the waiting yard is also full. Arrival intensity varies by hour of day.

Because arrival intensity changes by hour and service times are triangular/beta/PERT rather than exponential, there is no exact closed-form solution across the full day. This tab treats each hour as its own short-run steady state — an M/M/c/K-equivalent approximation using that hour's arrival rate and the average combined service time — to give a quick analytical benchmark. The simulation tabs capture the real dynamics exactly.
