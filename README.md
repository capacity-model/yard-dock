# Yard & Dock Doors Simulator

An interactive simulator of a yard with dock doors for truck loading and unloading: explore arrivals, dock capacity, service times, and congestion. Developed for **ISyE 6202 & 6335 — Supply Chain Facilities** at the Georgia Institute of Technology (Instructor: Prof. Benoit Montreuil).

- **Web (HTML):** https://capacity-model.github.io/yard-dock/

Runs entirely in the browser — nothing to install. Just open the link above, set the parameters, and run.
